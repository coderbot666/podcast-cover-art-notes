# Podcast Cover Art That Survives the Directory Grid

Podcast apps show covers at sizes from 55 to 300 pixels, surrounded by hundreds of other squares. Most covers are designed at 3000×3000 and never checked small. Here is the checklist I use with independent shows.

## Technical requirements
- 3000×3000 px, square, RGB, JPEG or PNG, under 512 KB for some hosts.
- No explicit-content signals if the show is marked clean. Some directories reject it.
- Avoid platform logos (Apple, Spotify) on the art. Directories reject covers that use them.

## The 55-pixel test
Export a 55×55 copy and put it in a grid of other covers in your category. Ask: can you read the show name? Do you know the topic? If not, simplify.

## One idea, two elements
A strong cover usually has the show name and one visual: a face, an object, or a symbol. Background texture is fine, but detailed scenes turn to mush at small sizes.

## Using generated imagery carefully
For a history show I worked on, we generated a textured parchment-and-ink background with [Seedream 5.5](https://seedream5-5.net/) and set the title in a real typeface on top. Generated lettering is still unreliable, so typography should always be added by hand.

## Series consistency
Episode artwork (if your host supports it) should reuse the cover's palette and layout so listeners recognise the show in their queue.
